Cat Bounce Bash

Dash, leap, and shimmy your way through a pastel obstacle course starring a fearless feline. Time the jumps over spin sticks, slide under swinging bars, and master the disco dance floor to reach the finish!

How to Play
  • Move: A/D or ←/→
  • Jump: W / ↑ / Space
  • Slide: S / ↓ / Shift
  • Dance: hit the arrow or letter shown inside the Disco Dome!
